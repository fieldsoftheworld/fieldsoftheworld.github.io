A global field boundary ecosystem

Fields of The World (FTW) is an open ecosystem for agricultural field boundary detection — combining a global benchmark dataset, baseline ML models, inference tools, and web applications.

Most countries lack complete maps of their agricultural fields. FTW uses open Sentinel-2 satellite imagery and machine learning to detect and map field boundaries at scale — supporting land use monitoring, food security assessment, and crop analysis worldwide.

Research
FTW Global Mapping
A first global agricultural field boundary map at 10m resolution — 3.17B polygons across 241 countries for 2024 and 2025, with a 500m confidence layer (AUC 0.82) and full-country F1 of 0.89 / 0.88 / 0.74 in Austria / Latvia / Finland. Robinson et al., Preprint 2026.
Read the paper
FTW Benchmark Dataset
A machine learning benchmark dataset for global agricultural field boundary segmentation — 1.6M+ labeled parcels across 24 countries on four continents, paired with multi-date Sentinel-2 imagery. Kerner et al., AAAI 2025.

FTW PRUE
A U-Net segmentation model with tiling-artifact mitigation, designed for large-scale deployment across Sentinel-2 mosaics. Muhawenayo et al., CVPR 2026.
